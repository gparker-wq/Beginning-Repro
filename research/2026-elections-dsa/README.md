# 2026 elections and the DSA: research record

Compiled 2026-11-04. All results are preliminary and none are certified. See the status notes in REPORT.md §0.

- **[REPORT.md](REPORT.md):** synthesis that answers all ten research questions and gives the four final conclusions.
- **[findings/](findings/):** the complete findings record. Each file has its own dated source ledger, a conflicts section and a not-found section.
  - `A_national.md`: Senate, House, leadership, turnout, historical comparison (A-01 to A-44)
  - `B_state_local.md`: state legislatures, ballot measures outside Washington, local races (B-1 to B-38)
  - `C_washington.md`: Washington measures, county splits, 26th LD, legislature, Congress (C-1 to C-45)
  - `D_dsa_roster.md`: DSA relationship matrix, race-by-race detail, win/loss analysis (D-01 to D-41)
  - `E_dsa_org_power.md`: DSA goals and structure, DNC contrast, networks, margin arithmetic (E-1 to E-25)
  - `F_voters_policy.md`: exit and voter polls, attitudes toward socialism, policy effects (F-1 to F-56)
  - `G_governors_gapfill.md`: governor races plus verification addendum (G-1 to G-25)

Earlier observations are kept, not overwritten. When a later report conflicts with an earlier one, both are recorded with their timestamps.
